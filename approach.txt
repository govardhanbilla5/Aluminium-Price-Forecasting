Problem: Given a part number, predict Price of this for the next 12 months

##Standard Quarters
JAN, FEB, MARCH, (1st Quarter) 
April, May, June (2nd Quarter)
July, August , September (3rd quarter)
Oct, Nov, December (4th Quarter)

Note:base/predicted Prices of any quarter will remain same
Example:
JAN, FEB, MARCH >> Same Price 
April, May, June >> Same Price
July, August , September >> Same price
Oct, Nov, December >> Same price


PPI Prediction for next 12 months from our model
LME Prediction for next 12 future months from our model
Midwest premium prediction for next 12 months will be fetched from the website(Already available)
CNG is constant that is 0.97
Drauss factor is constant that is 1.44


Let's assume we are sitting in Feb 2026

#####################################Prediction for March#############################################################################################

P_March  =  P_Feb  (As price will remain same for all months in a quarter)

#####################################Prediction for April(Formula work only for the new quarter)#########################################################

P_April  =  P_march(price of previous month/previous quarter)  +  { [ (AMS_Q)  −  (AMS_Q-1) ]  ×  PWt }  +  (PPI_Factor  ×  P_march)

AMS_Q  =  (MC_Q  ×  DF_c)  +  CNG_Q          |          PPI_Factor  =  (PPI_Q  −  PPI_Q-1)  /  PPI_Q-1


MC_Q = [(LME of current month(April) + Midwest of current month(April)]*0.91+ 1.25
MC_Q_1= [LME of last month of previous quarter(March) + Midwest of last month of previous quarter(March)]*0.91+1.25
PPI_Q=Average of current quarter of the month to be predicted(April, May, June)
PPI_Q-1=Average of previous quarter(Jan,Feb,March) or, last month value of previous quarter if historical value is present(March)
CNG_Q= Constant(0.97)
CNG_Q_1= Constant(0.97)
Pwt constant for each part
DF_c= constant(1.44)
P_current= we have already calculated for March in step 1


 


#############################################Prediction for May/june######################################################################################
P_April=P_May=P_June
NOTE:All the variables will also be same as of April

#################################################Prediction for July###################################################################################

P_July  =  P_(price of previous month i.e June/previous quarter)  +  { [ (AMS_Q)  −  (AMS_Q-1) ]  ×  PWt }  +  (PPI_Factor  ×  P_march)

AMS_Q  =  (MC_Q  ×  DF_c)  +  CNG_Q          |          PPI_Factor  =  (PPI_Q  −  PPI_Q-1)  /  PPI_Q-1


MC_Q = [(LME of current month(July) + Midwest of current month(July)]*0.91+ 1.25
MC_Q_1= [LME of last month of previous quarter(June) + Midwest of last month of previous quarter(June)]*0.91+1.25
PPI_Q=Average of current quarter(July, Aug, Sept)
PPI_Q-1=Average of previous quarter(April,May,june) or, last month value of previous quarter if historical value is present(June)
CNG_Q= Constant(0.97)
CNG_Q_1= Constant(0.97)
Pwt constant for each part
DF_c= constant(1.44)
P_current= we have already calculated for June(The price remains same as P_April=P_May=P_June)

#################################################Prediction for Aug/Sept############################################################################
P_July=P_Aug=P_Sept (As price will remain same for all months in a quarter)
NOTE:All the variables will also be same as of July
